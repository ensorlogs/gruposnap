Accessibility statement

Grupo Snap is committed to making its digital content accessible in accordance with WCAG 2.2 Level AA, to the extent reasonably achievable for a corporate site with content managed in WordPress and Elementor.

Measures implemented

  • Semantic structure with header, main content, and footer regions.
  • «Skip to main content» link visible when focused with the keyboard.
  • Floating accessibility panel: text size (3 levels), reading spacing, and high contrast, with preferences saved in your browser.
  • Enhanced visible focus on interactive controls.
  • Respect for prefers-reduced-motion when your system requests it.
  • Images with alternative text when provided in the editor.

Content under continuous improvement

Some imported blocks, carousels, or third-party embeds may not meet every criterion. We review and replace them when possible.

Found a barrier?

Write to us at contacto@gruposnap.com with the URL, the issue, and a screenshot if you can. We will respond within a reasonable time.

Compatibility

We test on recent versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge on desktop and mobile.
