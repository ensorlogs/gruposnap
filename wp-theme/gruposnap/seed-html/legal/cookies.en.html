1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small files stored on your device when you visit a site. We also use similar technologies such as localStorage to remember accessibility preferences and consent.

2. Types of cookies we use

  • Technical (always active). Essential for the site to work, to remember your consent, and accessibility panel preferences.
  • Analytics (optional). Aggregated data to understand which pages and products interest visitors most. They load only if you accept them.
  • Marketing (optional). May remember campaign interactions. Today they are not loaded by default; they would activate only with your prior consent.

4. How to manage your consent

On your first visit you will see a notice with Accept all, Reject all, or Customize. You can change your decision with Cookie preferences in the site footer.
